Every user story must use exactly this format:

## Story <N>: <Title>

**Description:** <1-3 sentences>

**Effort:** S | M | L

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- <criterion>
- <criterion>

**Dependencies:** <comma-separated story numbers this depends on, or "None">

Number stories sequentially starting from 1. Effort is exactly one of S, M, or L — no other
scale. Dependencies always has a value; write "None" rather than omitting the field.
